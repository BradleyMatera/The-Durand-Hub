A place worth coming together for

Rooted in history.
Made for what’s next.

In the heart of downtown Durand, two historic buildings are being given a new chapter. The vision of Bobbie and Bill Ziesk, The Durand Hub is imagined as a place where neighbors meet, visitors linger, and community takes center stage. Their current website describes a start-up café, bakery, and entertainment/gathering space projected to open in fall 2026.

From a quiet morning coffee to an evening worth celebrating, it's about more than what's on the menu. It's about creating a place that feels like it belongs to everyone.
